findphone

A Bluetooth phone finder for macOS.

Your phone is somewhere in the room. findphone turns your Mac's Bluetooth signal strength into a warmer-or-colder search: a speedometer that climbs as you get close, clicks that speed up, and an arrow that tells you which way to face. Macs have no Ultra-Wideband chip, so direction comes from signal trends and how your own body blocks the signal.

  • Native, no dependencies. A macOS app and command-line tool built only with Apple frameworks: CoreBluetooth, SwiftUI, AudioToolbox, WidgetKit and App Intents.
  • Steady readings from a noisy signal. A 7-sample median drives the dial and the click audio, so the needle doesn't jump around.
  • Direction without special hardware. A signal trend plus a guided four-way sweep, with a confidence rating.
  • Private by default. It only searches for a device you confirm is yours, never pairs, never shows device identifiers, and its --redact mode is checked by automated scans.
